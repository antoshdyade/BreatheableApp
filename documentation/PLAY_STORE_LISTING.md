## 2. Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Master your breath. Sharpen your daily focus. Build unshakable habits.**

BreatheLife is your all-in-one digital wellness sanctuary, uniting the proven science of conscious breathing biomechanics, educational Buteyko training, deep Pomodoro productivity cycles, and daily habit tracking into one seamless, privacy-first Android application.

Whether you need to dissolve acute workplace stress with Box Breathing, improve your educational breath-hold awareness with Buteyko Control Pause testing, maximize your work stamina with distraction-free Pomodoro sprints, or unwind into restorative sleep with soothing ocean soundscapes, BreatheLife empowers every moment of your day.

---

### KEY FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

🌿 **Comprehensive Breathing Library & Animated Guide**
• **Relaxation:** 4-7-8 Relaxing Breath, Box Breathing (Square 4-4-4-4), Coherent Resonance (5-5 cadence), Extended Exhalation (4-6).
• **Clarity & Focus:** Equal Breathing (Sama Vritti), Mindful Study Rhythm, Morning Awakening Cadence.
• **Diaphragmatic Mechanics:** Belly breath pacing engaging the lower ribs and vagus nerve.
• **Dynamic Breathing Orb:** Synchronized expanding/contracting visuals with gentle haptic vibration cues and Tibetan chime audio chimes.

🌬️ **Educational Buteyko Training Module**
• Self-recorded **Control Pause (CP)** timer to measure comfortable breath-hold baseline after a calm exhalation.
• Pulse rate tracking before and after practice.
• Educational wellness feedback and personalized progress notes.
• Strict non-diagnostic safety screening and medical wellness guidance.

⏱️ **Distraction-Free Pomodoro Productivity**
• 25-minute Deep Focus sprints followed by 5-minute restorative breaks and 15-minute long breaks.
• Automatic phase transitions and custom active task labels.
• Tibetan bell chimes and daily sprint counter.
• Prompts to take gentle nasal breathing breaks between work sessions.

🔥 **Daily Habit & Streak Consistency Tracker**
• Build positive daily routines: morning breathing, water intake, evening meditation, and posture walks.
• Real-time streak counters and personal best records.
• 7-day visual consistency heatmaps to celebrate small wins every day.
• Flexible scheduling and quick one-tap check-ins.

🧘 **Mindfulness Meditation & Soothing Ambient Soundscapes**
• Timed meditation sessions with gentle Tibetan bowl chimes.
• Built-in offline audio soundscapes: Gentle Rain, Ocean Surf, Forest Wind, or pure silence.
• Evening bedtime drift routine designed to calm a racing mind.

📊 **Unified Analytics & Full Local Data Privacy**
• Automatic activity history log tracking daily breathing minutes, focus sprints, and meditation sessions.
• **100% Offline & Private:** Zero account requirement, zero tracking, and zero ads. All logs reside safely in your device's local database.
• One-tap JSON data export and permanent wipe controls.
