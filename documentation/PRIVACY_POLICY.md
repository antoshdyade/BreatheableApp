## 1. Introduction

BreatheLife ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your personal privacy. BreatheLife is designed from the ground up as an offline-first wellness, breathing, meditation, and productivity companion. We believe your personal breathing habits, focus patterns, and journal notes belong solely to you.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
All information you create or enter into BreatheLife is stored exclusively within your device's local sandboxed database (Android Room database and SharedPreferences / DataStore):
- **Breathing Session History:** Durations, cycles completed, timestamps, and chosen breathing presets.
- **Educational Buteyko Practice Logs:** Self-recorded Control Pause (CP) seconds, pulse rate notes, and journal entries.
- **Productivity & Focus Records:** Pomodoro sprint completion counts and custom task titles.
- **Habit Tracker Data:** Habit titles, categories, completion dates, and streak statistics.
- **User Preferences:** Preferred wake-up and bedtime hours, haptic feedback toggles, and audio volume settings.

### B. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We **do not** collect personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
- We **do not** collect location data or GPS coordinates.
- We **do not** record microphone audio or use the camera.
- We **do not** transmit your wellness logs to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or analytics servers.
- We **do not** sell, monetize, or disclose your data to any third party.

## 3. Educational & Health Disclaimer
BreatheLife is an educational wellness and self-care application. **It is not a medical device, nor does it provide medical diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of any medical condition or pulmonary disease.** Control Pause (CP) times and breathing cadence guides are intended purely for recreational relaxation and educational awareness. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional regarding respiratory or cardiovascular concerns.

## 4. Permissions Used by the Application
BreatheLife only requests standard Android system permissions necessary to provide its core wellness features:
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: To show timely reminder chimes for scheduled breathing sessions and Pomodoro break notifications (Android 13+).
- `VIBRATE`: To provide gentle haptic cues during inhale/exhale cycles so users can breathe with eyes closed.
- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: To ensure local daily reminder alarms resume correctly after device restart.
- `WAKE_LOCK`: To keep the screen gently awake during an active breathing timer session.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all data is stored locally on your device:
- You retain complete ownership and control over your records at all times.
- You can export your full database anytime via the in-app Data Export feature.
- You can permanently wipe all records anytime via the in-app "Clear All Data" button, or by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

## 6. Children's Privacy
BreatheLife is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
