### What's New in Version 1.0.0

🎉 **Initial Official Release of BreatheLife**

- **Full Breathing Library:** Practice scientifically supported cadences including Box Breathing (4-4-4-4), 4-7-8 relaxing breath, Coherent 5-5 rhythm, diaphragmatic belly breathing, and equal breathing.
- **Dynamic Breathing Orb:** Synchronized visual pacing circle with soft haptic vibrations and Tibetan chime audio prompts.
- **Educational Buteyko Training:** Self-recorded Control Pause (CP) testing, pulse tracking, and educational guidance on functional nasal breathing.
- **Distraction-Free Pomodoro:** 25-minute focus sprints, 5-minute restorative breaks, and automatic long break transitions.
- **Habit Consistency Tracker:** Daily streak counts, 7-day visual consistency heatmaps, and customizable wellness habits.
- **Mindfulness Meditation & Ambient Soundscapes:** Offline synthesized audio chimes, gentle rain, and ocean surf timer modes.
- **100% Offline & Private:** Complete privacy without user tracking or mandatory accounts. All data is securely stored in your device's local database.
